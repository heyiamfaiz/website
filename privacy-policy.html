Who I am

This website at heyiamfaiz.com and all services are operated by Faiz, an independent Digital Strategist based in India. This policy explains how I handle both visitor information and client business data in compliance with the Information Technology Act, 2000 and India’s Digital Personal Data Protection DPDP Act, 2023.

1. Information I Collect

Website Visitors & Leads: When you visit this site or reach out to work with me, I collect basic details you share such as your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, brand name, and billing or GST details.

Technical & Analytics Data: Like most websites, this site uses cookies and tracking pixels including Google Analytics and Meta Pixel to collect standard technical data like your IP address, browser type, device info, and pages visited to improve site speed and measure marketing performance.

Client Store & Audit Data For Active Clients: When you hire me for a store build, managed hosting, or marketing partnership, I receive authorized access to your store backend on WooCommerce or Shopify, courier aggregator dashboards like Shiprocket or Delhivery, and ad accounts on Meta or Google strictly to execute your project and verify monthly delivered order volumes.

2. How Your Data is Used

Your information is used strictly to deliver, host, and optimize your e-commerce store and marketing campaigns, send official proposals, project updates, and invoices via email at contact@heyiamfaiz.com, coordinate day-to-day updates via phone or WhatsApp, verify monthly delivered order volumes and ad metrics for transparent billing reconciliation, and comply with Indian tax, accounting, and legal obligations.

3. E-Commerce Store Hosting & Customer Data (Data Processor Role)

Your Store’s Customers: When your e-commerce store is hosted on my managed cloud servers, your store collects order details including names, shipping addresses, and phone numbers from your buyers. Under the DPDP Act, 2023, you the client are the sole Data Fiduciary or Data Owner of your customers' data, and I act strictly as an infrastructure and technical facilitator.

Client Responsibility: You are solely responsible for maintaining a valid Privacy Policy on your own store and ensuring lawful consent for your own customer marketing via WhatsApp, SMS, or email campaigns.

Strict Confidentiality: I never sell, rent, extract, or share your customer lists, winning product data, supplier details, or store revenue numbers with any third party or competitor.

4. Third-Party Tools & Payments

Trusted Infrastructure: Limited data may be processed through trusted third-party infrastructure providers such as upstream cloud server hosts, Cloudflare security, email providers, and analytics platforms strictly as needed to run my services.

Payment Security: All payments are processed via secure, RBI-compliant banking channels like UPI, NEFT, or IMPS or third-party payment gateways like Razorpay. I never collect or store your raw credit or debit card numbers or banking passwords on my servers.

5. Your Rights & Data Retention

Your Rights: You have the right to update or correct your contact details, opt out of promotional messages, or request the deletion of your personal data by emailing contact@heyiamfaiz.com.

Legal & Tax Retention Exception: Please note that official email proposals, project communications, invoices, and transaction records cannot be deleted upon request where retention is legally required for tax compliance under Income Tax and GST laws or contract enforcement under the MSMED Act, 2006.

6. Data Security

I use industry-standard security measures including SSL encryption, server firewalls, restricted role access, and automated backups to keep data safe. However, because no internet transmission or cloud network is 100% immune to breaches, absolute security cannot be guaranteed.

7. Updates, Grievance Contact & Jurisdiction

This policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational or legal changes. Any privacy questions, data access requests, or grievances may be sent directly via email to contact@heyiamfaiz.com. This policy is governed by Indian law, and any disputes are subject to the exclusive jurisdiction of the courts in Delhi.

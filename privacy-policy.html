When you visit my website, contact me, or enquire about my services, I may collect information you provide, including your name, email address, phone or WhatsApp number, brand name, and billing or GST details where required.

I use information where necessary to build, host, maintain, and improve your website.

I access and handle this information only as reasonably necessary to provide the agreed hosting, development, maintenance, support, or marketing services, subject to applicable law and our agreement.

Refund Policy 💸

General Rule

Because calendar slots, dedicated cloud server instances, plugin licenses, and strategic roadmaps are reserved and provisioned immediately upon onboarding, all payments including website booking milestones, final delivery milestones, annual server or maintenance renewals, marketing retainers, and performance fees are strictly non-refundable once work has started.

Official Communication (Email Only)

In alignment with our Terms & Conditions, phone calls and WhatsApp are used strictly for fast day-to-day coordination. All official requests to cancel, pause, or review a billing milestone must be sent in writing via email to contact@heyiamfaiz.com. Verbal or WhatsApp requests are not valid.

24-Hour Cancellation Window

You may cancel within 24 hours of your initial payment for a full refund only if work has not started. Work is legally considered started the moment any server provisioning, domain or hosting setup, onboarding email dispatch, account access request, audit, planning, or execution activity begins.

Cancellation After Work Begins

E-Commerce Website Builds: If you choose to cancel or pause a website build after work has begun, the initial booking milestone is non-refundable as it covers dedicated server allocation, software licensing, and reserved development time. Once the staging link is delivered via email and deemed accepted after 3 business days as per our Terms, the final milestone payment is legally due and non-refundable.

Marketing Sprints & Monthly Retainers: Marketing partnerships require upfront strategic setup, tracking configuration, and testing. If you cancel during an initial lock-in sprint or an active monthly billing cycle, deliverables will conclude at the end of the current paid period, and no full or prorated refunds are issued for unused days or remaining sprint cycles.

Annual Server & Maintenance Renewals: Annual hosting and technical maintenance renewals are non-refundable once the renewal date passes and server instances are extended for the new term.

Third-Party Ad Spend: Because you pay Meta, Google, and other advertising networks directly from your own bank account or card, I never collect, hold, or refund third-party ad budgets.

Pausing Services Instead of Cancelling

Ongoing marketing services may be paused once for up to 45 consecutive days by submitting a written request via email to contact@heyiamfaiz.com. Any prepaid retainer amount is held safely as non-refundable service credit, which must be utilized within 90 days of the original payment date.

What Refunds Do NOT Cover

Refunds are strictly not provided for market and ROI fluctuations like failure to achieve specific sales volume, net profit, or ROAS targets since market demand, product pricing, courier delivery and RTO rates, and third-party ad algorithms are outside my control. They also do not cover subjective changes in your business direction or design preferences after a layout is approved, client-side delays like missing product photos or unshared logins, or third-party issues like account bans or platform downtime.

When a Refund WILL Apply (My 14-Day Guarantee to You)

A refund for your current active milestone will be granted only if I fail to initiate your project within 14 consecutive days after receiving both your cleared payment and all required onboarding assets or logins via email, or if I remain completely inactive and unresponsive for 14 consecutive business days without prior notice excluding delays caused by pending client inputs. This inactivity clause does not apply during unavoidable force majeure events beyond human control such as medical emergencies, natural disasters, internet shutdowns, or cloud outages.

Payment Disputes & MSMED Act Protection

Raising an unauthorized payment gateway dispute or bank or UPI chargeback after work has commenced or deliverables have been shared via email constitutes a breach of contract.

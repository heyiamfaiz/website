Refund Policy 💸

Last updated: 19 October 2026

I believe in being transparent about payments and refunds. This policy explains when you can cancel a service, when a refund may be available, and how to request one.

Please read this policy alongside my Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy .

1. Our Refund Approach

I reserve time, tools, hosting resources, and expertise for every project. Refund eligibility depends on when you cancel, the work completed, and the terms of your proposal.

Payments are not automatically refundable once work begins. However, a refund may be available in the situations explained below, subject to applicable law.

2. Cancelling Within 24 Hours

You may request a full refund within 24 hours of your initial payment if work has not started.

Work is considered started when I begin project-specific activities such as planning, research, audits, account setup, hosting setup, tracking configuration, or development.

To request a cancellation, email contact@heyiamfaiz.com .

3. Cancelling After Work Begins

Website Development

The booking payment generally becomes non-refundable once project-specific work begins. If the agreed staging website is delivered, the remaining payment is due according to your written proposal.

If you cancel after work begins, completed work, agreed milestones, and applicable costs will be accounted for under your agreement and applicable law.

Marketing Services & Monthly Retainers

Payments cover the services and billing period agreed in your proposal. If you cancel during an initial commitment period or an active billing cycle, the agreed fees for that period remain payable, subject to applicable law and your contract.

Unused days are not automatically refundable.

Hosting & Maintenance

Annual hosting and maintenance renewals are generally non-refundable once the new service period begins, subject to applicable law.

Advertising Budgets

Advertising spend is paid directly to platforms such as Meta and Google. Refunds for those amounts must be requested from the relevant platform.

4. When I Will Offer a Refund

You may qualify for a refund of the affected milestone if:

  • I fail to initiate your project within 14 consecutive days after receiving your cleared payment and all required onboarding information and access.
  • I remain completely inactive and unresponsive for 14 consecutive business days without prior notice, excluding delays caused by missing information or access from your side.

In these situations, I will review the work completed and the applicable agreement to determine the appropriate refund for the affected milestone.

Unavoidable events, such as natural disasters, serious emergencies, or major infrastructure outages, may reasonably extend project timelines.

5. Pausing Marketing Services

You may request one pause of up to 45 consecutive days for an ongoing marketing service by emailing contact@heyiamfaiz.com .

Any unused prepaid amount may be held as service credit, subject to written confirmation. The credit must be used within 90 days of the original payment date unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by law.

6. Situations That Do Not Automatically Qualify for a Refund

A refund is not automatically due because of:

  • Lower-than-expected sales, profits, or advertising returns.
  • Changes in your business direction or preferences after approving a deliverable.
  • Delays caused by missing content, feedback, access, or verification from your side.
  • Third-party account restrictions, advertising rejections, payment holds, or courier delays outside my reasonable control.

These rules do not remove any refund rights you may have under applicable law.

7. Payment Disputes

If you believe a payment is incorrect or unauthorised, contact me promptly so we can review it.

Please do not make a false payment dispute for work you authorised and received. Legitimate payment disputes and your rights under applicable law remain unaffected.

Outstanding eligible invoices may be pursued through the remedies available under the contract and applicable law, including the MSMED Act where applicable.

8. How to Request a Refund

Email contact@heyiamfaiz.com from your project email address and include:

  • Your name and invoice number.
  • The service you purchased.
  • The reason for your request.

I will review your request and aim to respond within five business days. This is a response target, not a guarantee that every request will qualify for a refund.

9. Contact

Have a question about this policy? Email me at contact@heyiamfaiz.com .

You can also read my Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy .

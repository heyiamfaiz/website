Refund Policy 💸

Last updated: 19 October 2026

This policy explains when you can cancel a service, when a refund may be available, and how to request one. Please read it alongside my Terms & Conditions and Privacy Policy.

1. General Refund Rule

I reserve time, hosting resources, software licences, and strategic work for each client. Payments are generally non-refundable once project-specific work has started, subject to the refund conditions below and applicable law.

This applies to website booking payments, final delivery milestones, annual hosting and maintenance renewals, marketing retainers, and performance fees that have already been earned.

2. Official Cancellation Requests

All official cancellation, pause, and billing review requests must be sent by email to contact@heyiamfaiz.com. WhatsApp messages and calls may be used for coordination, but the request must be confirmed by email.

3. Cancellation Within 24 Hours

You may request a full refund within 24 hours of your initial payment, provided project-specific work has not started.

Work is considered started when I begin activities such as server provisioning, domain or hosting setup, onboarding, account access requests, audits, research, planning, or execution.

4. Cancellation After Work Begins

E-commerce website builds

Once project-specific work begins, the booking payment is generally non-refundable because it covers reserved development time, setup, and applicable resources. Once the staging website is delivered, the final milestone becomes due according to the Terms & Conditions and your written proposal.

If you cancel after work has started, the amounts payable and any refund due will be assessed against completed work, agreed milestones, and applicable law.

Marketing sprints and monthly retainers

Marketing services may involve strategy, tracking setup, testing, and ongoing optimisation. If you cancel during an initial commitment period or an active monthly billing cycle, the agreed fees for that period remain payable, subject to applicable law and your contract. Unused days or remaining sprint time are not automatically refundable.

Annual hosting and maintenance

Annual renewals are generally non-refundable once the new service period begins, subject to applicable law.

Third-party advertising spend

Advertising budgets are paid directly by you to platforms such as Meta and Google. I do not collect or hold these advertising budgets, so requests for their refund must be made to the relevant platform.

5. Pausing Marketing Services

You may request one pause of up to 45 consecutive days for an ongoing marketing service by emailing contact@heyiamfaiz.com.

Any unused prepaid retainer amount may be held as non-refundable service credit, subject to written confirmation. The credit must be used within 90 days of the original payment date unless otherwise agreed in writing or required by law.

6. Situations That Do Not Automatically Qualify for a Refund

A refund is not automatically due because of lower-than-expected sales, profits, or return on ad spend (ROAS). Marketing results depend on market demand, pricing, advertising algorithms, and courier delivery or RTO rates.

A refund is not automatically due because of changes in your business direction, products, or design preferences after a deliverable has been approved.

A refund is not automatically due for delays caused by missing product photos, text, feedback, access, GST information, payment gateway KYC, or courier verification from your side.

A refund is not automatically due for third-party account bans, advertising rejections, payment holds, or service interruptions outside my reasonable control.

These conditions do not remove any rights you may have under applicable law.

7. When a Refund Will Apply: 14-Day Commitment

You may qualify for a refund of the affected active milestone if either of the following occurs: I fail to initiate your project within 14 consecutive days after receiving your cleared payment and all required onboarding assets and access.

You may also qualify if I remain completely inactive and unresponsive for 14 consecutive business days without prior notice, excluding delays caused by pending information or access from your side.

If either condition applies, I will review the work completed and the applicable agreement to determine the refund due for the affected milestone.

Unavoidable events: These inactivity conditions may be affected by events beyond reasonable human control, such as serious medical emergencies, natural disasters, government-mandated internet shutdowns, or major cloud infrastructure outages. In such cases, timelines may be reasonably extended.

8. Payment Disputes

If you believe a payment is incorrect or unauthorised, contact me promptly so we can review it. Please do not make a false payment dispute for work you authorised and received.

Legitimate disputes and your rights under applicable law remain unaffected. Eligible unpaid invoices may be pursued through contractual and statutory recovery processes, including the MSMED Act where applicable.

9. How to Request a Refund

Email contact@heyiamfaiz.com from your project email address and include your name, invoice number, service purchased, and reason for the request.

I aim to review and respond to qualifying requests within five business days. This is a response target, not a guarantee that every request will qualify for a refund.

Contact

For cancellation, billing, or refund questions, contact contact@heyiamfaiz.com.
